About

Mechanical systems, from tolerance stack-up to shop floor.

I'm an aerospace and mechanical engineering student. I care more about whether something works than what discipline it falls under. As Chief Engineer for UB's University Rover Challenge team, I lead cross-functional integration across mechanical, electrical, and software subteams. My background is hardware-first, so I'm usually the one making sure CAD, code, and machining actually line up. I do similar work as a Manufacturing Engineering Intern at Moog, building tooling and documentation that carries design intent from CAD to the shop floor. Either way, the core skill is the same: understand how the physical system behaves, then build the process or software around it.
